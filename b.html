01Do I need a developer to update content?

No. Use the visual editor to update content directly on your site. Your team can manage everyday changes and publish without waiting on a developer.

02Can different audiences see different content?

Yes. You can tailor content for different audiences within the same site, giving each audience a more relevant experience.

03Can my team approve changes before publishing?

Yes. Your team can review changes and approve content before it goes live, keeping you in control from the first edit to the final approval.
